Overview

PlantNetX is a comprehensive gene expression database in rice, built upon an extensive collection of RNA-Seq data derived from various tissues and organs at different stages of growth and development under natural field conditions, as well as rice plants subjected to various treatments. The RNA-Seq data utilized for coexpression analysis is accessible within PlantNetX, a repository of gene expression profiles obtained through RNA sequencing. PlantNetX aims to provide a robust platform for the identification of functionally related genes in diverse biological pathways and/or metabolic processes.
